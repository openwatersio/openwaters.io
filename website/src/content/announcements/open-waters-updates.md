Welcome to Open Waters [Updates](/updates/), a feed of all our project releases and announcements. If you only care about one topic, you can filter releases and announcements by project: [AIS](/updates/ais/), [Tides & Currents](/updates/tides/), [Charts](/updates/charts/), and [Sky](/updates/sky/). You can also show only [announcements](/updates/announcements/) or only [releases](/updates/releases/).

Every page has an RSS feed. Add [the announcements feed](/updates/announcements/rss.xml) to your reader if you only want the big news, or a project's release feed, such as [AIS releases](/updates/ais/releases/rss.xml), if you build on it.

Questions or ideas? Find us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/NeMyrYMGr).
