Welcome to the Open Waters [changelog](/changelog/), a feed of all our project releases and announcements. If you only care about one topic, you can filter releases and announcements by project: [AIS](/changelog/ais/), [Tides & Currents](/changelog/tides/), [Charts](/changelog/charts/), and [Sky](/changelog/sky/). You can also show only [announcements](/changelog/announcements/) or only [releases](/changelog/releases/).

Every page has an RSS feed. Add [the announcements feed](/changelog/announcements/rss.xml) to your reader if you only want the big news, or a project's release feed, such as [AIS releases](/changelog/ais/releases/rss.xml), if you build on it.

Questions or ideas? Find us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/NeMyrYMGr).
